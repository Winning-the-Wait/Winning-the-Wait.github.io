KNOW YOUR ENEMIES

Meet the Villains of Queues

Every queue has its troublemakers. Some create chaos by making arrivals and service times unpredictable. Others turn high utilization into crippling congestion. Still others leave the actual wait unchanged but make it feel much longer. Meet Randomizer, Chain Master, and Time Bender: three villains who make life difficult for customers and managers alike. To become a Queue Whisperer, you must first learn to recognize their tricks, measure their powers, and develop effective strategies to defeat them.

Villain 1 of 3

Randomizer

The Master of Variability

Randomizer loves surprises—and not the pleasant kind. He makes customers arrive in unpredictable bursts and turns routine service into a guessing game. One customer takes seconds; the next takes forever. Even when average demand and capacity stay the same, this variability can create longer queues and less predictable waits.

NOTABLE QUOTE:
  • Smooth operations? Over my probabilistic dead body.
  • Every outcome is possible. Especially the terrible ones.
  • I never gamble. I always win.
Villain 2 of 3

Chain Master

The Lord of Dependence

Chain Master is the villain who links everyone in a queue together, making your wait depend on the customers ahead of you. Unlike Randomizer, who creates chaos through unpredictability, Chain Master forges invisible chains between customers, forcing the entire line to move at the pace of those being served. You may be ready for lightning-fast service, but if the person ahead is fumbling with coupons, searching for a wallet, or asking endless questions, Chain Master tightens his grip and brings you to a halt. Under his power, your waiting time is no longer entirely your own—it is chained to the people ahead of you.

NOTABLE QUOTE:
  • One person’s pause becomes everyone’s prison.
  • Independence is chaos, chains create order.
  • My chains are invisible. Their consequences aren’t.
Villain 3 of 3

Time Bender

The Manipulator of Perceived Time

Time Bender is a master illusionist who cannot change the clock on the wall but can manipulate the clock inside your mind. While the actual clock says only four minutes have passed, your brain insists it has been fourteen. His secret weapon is human emotion. By exploiting boredom, anxiety, uncertainty, and frustration, he accelerates our internal clock, making waiting feel much longer than it actually is. Under Time Bender’s spell, the real enemy is not the time we spend waiting, but how we experience it.

NOTABLE QUOTE:
  • I don’t add time, I add weight to every minute.
  • I turn uncertainty into eternity.
  • The clock measures time. I control how you feel.

Every Villain Has a Weakness

Randomizer can be challenged by reducing unnecessary variability. Chain Master can be weakened through thoughtful capacity planning and by leaving room for demand fluctuations. And Time Bender loses his power when customers receive clear information, meaningful progress, and a better waiting experience. Recognizing these villains is the first step toward designing shorter lines and happier customers. Winning the Wait shows you how.
