About the Author

Research, teaching, and the ideas behind Winning the Wait.

Seyed Iravani

Seyed Iravani is a Professor of Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences at Northwestern University's McCormick School of Engineering and holds a courtesy appointment as Professor of Operations at the Kellogg School of Management. His research applies stochastic processes, game theory, social networks, and queueing theory to the design and management of manufacturing systems, service operations, healthcare, supply chains, and nonprofit organizations, with an emphasis on improving flexibility, coordination, and responsiveness.

For nearly three decades, he has taught operations management and queueing theory to undergraduate, PhD, and MBA students, while collaborating with industries ranging from manufacturing and hospitality to healthcare and other service sectors.

Winning the Wait reflects his distinctive teaching approach: making complex operations concepts accessible through intuitive explanations, practical examples, and engaging storytelling. Drawing on decades of research, teaching, and industry experience, the book helps readers understand why queues form, why waits grow long, and how managers can design systems that deliver shorter lines, smoother operations, and happier customers.
